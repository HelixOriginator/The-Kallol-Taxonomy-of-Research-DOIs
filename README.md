# The Kallol Taxonomy of Research DOIs
### A Framework for High-Velocity Independent Scholarship

Traditional academic models often fail to account for the speed and diversity of modern independent research. This repository introduces the **Three-Tier Taxonomy**, a methodological framework designed to categorize Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs) based on their functional role in the research lifecycle.

Developed by the **Helix Originator**, this taxonomy serves as the foundational logic for managing a verified portfolio of 260+ research and policy works.

---

## The Three Tiers of Research Attribution

### Tier 1: The Dissemination DOI (Rapid Knowledge Transfer)
* **Functional Role:** Protecting "First-to-Market" ideas, Ready Reckoners, Policy Briefs, and Framework Outlines.
* **Objective:** Prioritizes the **Velocity of Knowledge** and immediate public accessibility.
* **Context:** Vital for real-time policy innovation where traditional publication cycles are too slow to address urgent civic needs.

### Tier 2: The Infrastructure DOI (Evidence & Civic Trust)
* **Functional Role:** Archiving Raw Datasets, Legal Architectures, Survey Results, and Evidence Building Blocks.
* **Objective:** Establishes **Transparency and Reproducibility**.
* **Context:** Provides the "Sovereign Data Foundation" required for third-party auditing, government verification, and Limca Book of Records documentation.

### Tier 3: The Validation DOI (Methodological Scrutiny)
* **Functional Role:** Theoretical Deep-Dives, Comparative Studies, and Peer-Reviewed Analysis.
* **Objective:** Provides **Academic Benchmarking** and rigorous expert critique.
* **Context:** Represents the final layer of institutional integration for mature research concepts.

---

## The "Integrity by Design" Approach
By distinguishing between these tiers, independent researchers can ensure that high-velocity dissemination (Tier 1) is recognized as a legitimate, high-impact scientific activity. This system proves that **Volume + Variety = A Complete Ecosystem of Knowledge.**
